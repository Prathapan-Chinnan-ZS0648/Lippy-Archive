## 1. Introduction & Background

Ashford Financial Group (“AFG” or “the Company”) is a regional bank holding company operating 55 branch locations across six states (Pennsylvania, Ohio, New York, New Jersey, Delaware, and Maryland), with approximately 2,650 employees, following the February 2026 acquisition of Colonial Trust Bank, which added 9 branch locations in Maryland. AFG's current IT ticketing platform, TrackIT Legacy, was deployed in 2014 and no longer meets the Company's needs for workflow automation, self-service, and reporting. AFG is reissuing this Request for Proposal (RFP) to solicit qualified vendors to replace its legacy ticketing platform with a modern IT Service Management (ITSM) solution, provide outsourced Tier 1 and Tier 2 helpdesk support services, and establish a formal Major Incident Response capability, following a rise in Severity 1 outages observed across the combined AFG and Colonial Trust environment in the first quarter of 2026.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| ITSM | IT Service Management — the platform and processes used to manage IT service requests, incidents, and changes. |
| Helpdesk | The vendor-operated support function that receives, triages, and resolves employee IT service requests. |
| SLA | Service Level Agreement — a contractually defined performance commitment. |
| TAM | Technical Account Manager — the vendor's designated point of contact for service escalations. |
| FFIEC | Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council — issuer of interagency guidance applicable to bank technology vendors. |
| MSA | Master Services Agreement — the overarching contract governing the vendor relationship. |

## 3. Project Objectives

- Replace the legacy TrackIT ticketing platform with a modern, ITIL v4-aligned ITSM solution.
- Establish outsourced Tier 1 and Tier 2 helpdesk support to improve first-contact resolution rates.
- Reduce average ticket resolution time by at least 25% within 12 months of go-live.
- Provide employees with a modern self-service portal and knowledge base.
- Maintain compliance with applicable FFIEC guidance on third-party technology service providers throughout the engagement.
- Reduce Major Incident (Severity 1) mean-time-to-resolution by at least 25% within 12 months of go-live. [NEW]

## 4. Scope of Work

1. Design, configure, and deploy a replacement ITSM platform covering incident, request, problem, and change management.
2. Provide outsourced Tier 1 and Tier 2 helpdesk support for approximately 2,650 AFG employees across 55 branch and corporate locations.
