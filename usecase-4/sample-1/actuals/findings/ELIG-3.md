# ELIG-3

**7. Eligibility Criteria** — CHANGED (material)

## Old
> At least 3 reference implementations for distribution networks operating facilities of 500,000+ square feet, completed within the past 5 years.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S1_ORIGINAL.pdf, page 6

## New
> At least 5 reference implementations for distribution networks operating facilities of 500,000+ square feet, completed within the past 5 years, of which at least 2 must include refrigerated/cold chain logistics.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S1_REVISED.pdf, page 7

## What changed
Reference count raised 3 → 5, with a new sub-requirement that at least 2 involve cold chain/refrigerated logistics.

## Why it matters
A vendor without cold-chain reference projects is now ineligible regardless of general WMS experience; a materially narrower vendor pool.
