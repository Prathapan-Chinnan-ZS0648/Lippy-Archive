# ELIG-5

**7. Eligibility Criteria** — CHANGED (material)

## Old
> Minimum insurance coverage: $3,000,000 general liability; $1,000,000 professional liability/E&O.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S1_ORIGINAL.pdf, page 6

## New
> Minimum insurance coverage: $6,000,000 general liability; $2,000,000 professional liability/E&O; $3,000,000 cyber liability.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S1_REVISED.pdf, page 7

## What changed
General liability and professional liability minimums doubled, and a new $3M cyber liability minimum added.

## Why it matters
Increases the vendor's insurance cost of doing business and adds a coverage type (cyber) some vendors may not carry; mirrors INS-1–INS-3.
