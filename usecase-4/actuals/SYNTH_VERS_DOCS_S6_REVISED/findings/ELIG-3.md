# ELIG-3
**7. Eligibility Criteria — CHANGED**

## Old
> At least 4 reference implementations for state or provincial DOT ITS deployments covering 500 or more field devices, completed within the past 7 years.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_ORIGINAL.pdf, 7. Eligibility Criteria, page 9

## New
> At least 5 reference implementations for state or provincial DOT ITS deployments covering 500 or more field devices, completed within the past 7 years, of which at least 2 must include a 24/7 SOC monitoring component. [MODIFIED]
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_REVISED.pdf, 7. Eligibility Criteria, page 9

## What changed
The reference-implementation minimum rises from 4 to 5, and at least 2 references must now include a 24/7 SOC monitoring component.

## Why it matters
A vendor with strong ITS references but no SOC monitoring reference, sufficient under the original, would fail this criterion under the revision.
