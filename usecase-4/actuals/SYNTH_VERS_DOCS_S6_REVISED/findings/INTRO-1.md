# INTRO-1
**1. Introduction & Background — CHANGED**

## Old
> The Cascadia Department of Transportation ("CDOT" or "the Department") operates and maintains a statewide Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) network spanning approximately 1,450 centerline miles of interstate and state highways. The network includes 620 closed-circuit television (CCTV) cameras, 340 dynamic message signs (DMS), and 180 ramp metering locations, all connected to a Traffic Management Center (TMC) located in Cascadia City.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_ORIGINAL.pdf, 1. Introduction & Background, page 3

## New
> The Cascadia Department of Transportation ("CDOT" or "the Department") operates and maintains a statewide Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) network spanning approximately 1,464 centerline miles of interstate and state highways, following the February 2027 opening of the SR-509 Port Neelan Extension. The network now includes 660 closed-circuit television (CCTV) cameras, 355 dynamic message signs (DMS), and 196 ramp metering locations, all connected to a Traffic Management Center (TMC) located in Cascadia City.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_REVISED.pdf, 1. Introduction & Background, page 3

## What changed
Network mileage rises from 1,450 to 1,464 centerline miles following the SR-509 Port Neelan Extension opening; device counts rise across all three categories (CCTV 620→660, DMS 340→355, ramp meters 180→196).

## Why it matters
Establishes the scale increase behind every downstream device-count change in this pack (Scope of Work, Appendix A, Appendix E).
