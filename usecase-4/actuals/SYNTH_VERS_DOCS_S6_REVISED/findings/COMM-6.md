# COMM-6
**12. Commercial Terms — CHANGED**

## Old
> Liquidated damages: 0.25% of total contract value per week of delay against the approved schedule, capped at 8% of total contract value.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_ORIGINAL.pdf, 12. Commercial Terms, page 11

## New
> Liquidated damages: 0.35% of total contract value per week of delay against the approved schedule, capped at 10% of total contract value. [MODIFIED]
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_REVISED.pdf, 12. Commercial Terms, page 11

## What changed
The liquidated-damages rate rises from 0.25% to 0.35% of total contract value per week, and the cap rises from 8% to 10%.

## Why it matters
Both a higher weekly rate and a higher cap increase a vendor's schedule-risk exposure, on top of a much larger contract value (COMM-1) the percentage is calculated against.
