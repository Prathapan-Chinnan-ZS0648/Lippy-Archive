# ELIG-6
**7. Eligibility Criteria — CHANGED**

## Old
> Minimum insurance coverage: $10,000,000 general liability; $5,000,000 professional liability/E&O.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_ORIGINAL.pdf, 7. Eligibility Criteria, page 9

## New
> Minimum insurance coverage: $15,000,000 general liability; $8,000,000 professional liability/E&O; $5,000,000 cyber liability. [MODIFIED]
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_REVISED.pdf, 7. Eligibility Criteria, page 9

## What changed
General liability rises from $10M to $15M, professional liability from $5M to $8M, and a new $5M cyber liability minimum is added.

## Why it matters
A binding eligibility floor; mirrors the Section 11 insurance changes at INS-1, INS-2, and the new INS-3 cyber liability requirement.
