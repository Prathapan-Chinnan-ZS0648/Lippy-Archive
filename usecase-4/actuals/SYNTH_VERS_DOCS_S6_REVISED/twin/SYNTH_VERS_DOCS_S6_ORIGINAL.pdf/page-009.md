## 7. Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for consideration, vendors must meet all of the following minimum criteria:

- Minimum of 8 years of experience providing ITS/ATMS systems integration services to state or provincial transportation agencies.
- Minimum annual revenue of $40,000,000 (audited financials for the most recent fiscal year required).
- At least 4 reference implementations for state or provincial DOT ITS deployments covering 500 or more field devices, completed within the past 7 years.
- A Professional Engineer (PE) licensed in the state, on staff, responsible for ITS system design.
- Minimum insurance coverage: $10,000,000 general liability; $5,000,000 professional liability/E&O.
- Performance bond equal to 100% of the awarded contract value.
