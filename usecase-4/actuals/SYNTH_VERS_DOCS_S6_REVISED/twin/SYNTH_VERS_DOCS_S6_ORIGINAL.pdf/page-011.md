## 10. Evaluation Criteria

Proposals will be scored on a 100-point scale as follows:

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Technical Solution & Architecture | 25 points |
| Vendor Experience & References | 20 points |
| Implementation Approach & Timeline | 15 points |
| Cost | 25 points |
| Cybersecurity Approach | 10 points |
| O&M / Support Plan | 5 points |

## 11. Insurance & Bonding Requirements

- Commercial General Liability: $10,000,000 per occurrence.
- Professional Liability / Errors & Omissions: $5,000,000 per claim.
- Performance Bond: 100% of total contract value, issued by a surety rated A- or better by A.M. Best.
- CDOT and its officers, agents, and employees must be named as additional insureds on all policies except professional liability.

## 12. Commercial Terms

Estimated Project Budget: $85,000,000 – $110,000,000

Contract Term: 6 years, with two (2) optional 12-month renewals.

Payment Schedule
- 10% upon contract signing / NTP
- 15% upon System Design Document acceptance
- 20% upon Phase 1 Pilot Corridor go-live
- 25% upon Phase 2 Regional Rollout go-live
- 20% upon Phase 3 Statewide Rollout go-live
- 10% upon final acceptance

Other Commercial Terms
- Warranty period: 3 years following each phase's go-live, covering defect remediation at no additional cost.
- Payment terms: Net 45 from invoice date.
- Liquidated damages: 0.25% of total contract value per week of delay against the approved schedule, capped at 8% of total contract value.
