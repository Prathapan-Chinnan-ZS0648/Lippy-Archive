## 7. Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for consideration, vendors must meet all of the following minimum criteria:

- Minimum of 10 years of experience providing ITS/ATMS systems integration services to state or provincial transportation agencies. [MODIFIED]
- Minimum annual revenue of $60,000,000 (audited financials for the most recent fiscal year required). [MODIFIED]
- At least 5 reference implementations for state or provincial DOT ITS deployments covering 500 or more field devices, completed within the past 7 years, of which at least 2 must include a 24/7 SOC monitoring component. [MODIFIED]
- A Professional Engineer (PE) licensed in the state, on staff, responsible for ITS system design.
- Vendor's SOC personnel must hold current CISSP or GIAC certification. [NEW]
- Minimum insurance coverage: $15,000,000 general liability; $8,000,000 professional liability/E&O; $5,000,000 cyber liability. [MODIFIED]
- Performance bond equal to 100% of the awarded contract value.
