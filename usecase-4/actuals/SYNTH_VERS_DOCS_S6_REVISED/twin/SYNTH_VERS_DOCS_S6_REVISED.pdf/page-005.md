## 4. Scope of Work

1. Design, procure, and deploy a replacement Advanced Traffic Management System (ATMS) software platform.
2. Replace 660 end-of-life CCTV cameras with new pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) IP cameras across all corridors listed in Appendix E. [MODIFIED — was 620]
3. Replace or upgrade 355 dynamic message sign (DMS) controllers, retaining existing sign structures where feasible. [MODIFIED — was 340]
4. Modernize 196 ramp metering locations with adaptive, demand-responsive metering algorithms. [MODIFIED — was 180]
5. Upgrade the fiber and wireless communications backbone connecting all field devices to the TMC, including the new SR-509 corridor.
6. Integrate the new ATMS platform with CDOT's statewide 511 traveler information system.
7. Implement AI-based automated incident detection algorithms across all CCTV-covered corridors.
8. Provide TMC operator training for approximately 52 operators across three shifts. [MODIFIED — was 45]
9. Harden ITS network cybersecurity in alignment with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.
10. Establish 24/7 Security Operations Center (SOC) monitoring of the ITS communications network, including automated threat detection and incident response. [NEW]
11. Provide 5 years of post-deployment operations and maintenance (O&M) support.
