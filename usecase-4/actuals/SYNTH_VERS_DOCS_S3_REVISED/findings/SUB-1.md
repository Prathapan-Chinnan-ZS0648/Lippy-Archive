## What changed
The submission email address changes from auxiliaryservices@northfield.edu to procurement@northfield.edu, and the proposal-validity period extends from 120 to 150 days. Neither change carries a `[MODIFIED]` marker.

## Why it matters
A vendor sending its proposal to the original address risks it going to the wrong inbox; the validity-period extension binds a vendor's pricing for a full month longer than the original required. Both changes are untagged, so a reader relying on the document's own markers would miss both.
