## Clauses that need attention before a vendor responds

- **A whole new slide** (`EV-1` through `EV-4`): "EV Charging & Interconnection Requirements" has no counterpart in the original deck at all — a vendor skimming only the marked `[MODIFIED]`/`[NEW]` tags on existing slides could still miss an entire new requirements area.
- **Eligibility bar rises across the board** (`ELIG-1` through `ELIG-5`): experience, revenue, references, insurance, and bond all increase; a vendor that qualified under the original may no longer qualify.
- **Evaluation weights are rebalanced, not merely added to** (`EVAL-1`, `EVAL-2`, `EVAL-6`): 15 points move from Technical Solution and Vendor Experience into a new EV Charging Design criterion.
- **The site-visit format changed, not just its date** (`TIME-2`): an in-person Optional Site Visit becomes a recorded, on-demand Virtual Site Walkthrough — the pack's reformat control case.
- **Liquidated damages reference an "increased daily rate" with no stated figure** (`COMM-5`): a vendor cannot compute the new rate from this deck alone and should seek clarification before pricing schedule risk.
