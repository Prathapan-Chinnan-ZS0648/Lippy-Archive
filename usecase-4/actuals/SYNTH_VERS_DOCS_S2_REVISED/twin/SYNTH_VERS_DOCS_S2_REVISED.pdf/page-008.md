## 8. Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for consideration, vendors must meet all of the following minimum criteria:

- Minimum of 7 years of experience providing ITSM platforms and/or managed helpdesk services to financial institutions. [MODIFIED]
- Minimum annual revenue of $12,000,000 (audited financials for the most recent fiscal year required). [MODIFIED]
- At least 4 reference implementations for organizations with 1,500 or more employees, completed within the past 5 years.
- Vendor must demonstrate prior experience operating a formal Major Incident Management process for a financial services client. [NEW]
- Minimum insurance coverage: $3,000,000 general liability; $2,000,000 professional liability/E&O; $2,000,000 cyber liability.
