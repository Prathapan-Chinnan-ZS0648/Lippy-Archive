## Clauses that need attention before a vendor responds

These are the changes most likely to gate or reshape a vendor's response, drawn from the material=yes rows above:

- **Eligibility bar raised** (ELIG-1, ELIG-2, ELIG-4): 7 years' experience (was 5), $12M revenue (was $8M), prior Major Incident Management experience required (new) — reference count and insurance minimums are unchanged.
- **Section 6 removed outright** (ONB-1–ONB-5, RES-1): all five vendor onboarding/staffing requirements are gone from the RFP text and now live exclusively in the Master Services Agreement — a vendor who only reads the RFP will miss them.
- **Hosting model narrowed** (TECH-9): on-premise is no longer an option — cloud-only.
- **New Major Incident Response workstream** (TECH-5, TECH-19, SCOPE-6, FR-07/08, DEL-6, TC-2, EVAL-5): a materially new operational and compliance scope, not an incremental tweak.
- **New SOC 2 Type II certification requirement** (TECH-14, DEL-7, APPC-5): a technical/compliance gate some vendors may not currently meet.
- **New ongoing Service Level Credit Schedule** (SLC-1–SLC-3): financial exposure that continues into the operational period, separate from the implementation-phase liquidated damages (COMM-6).
- **Commercial terms shifted against the vendor's cash flow** (COMM-3): payment schedule restructured to less upfront/final and more milestone-gated.
- **Helpdesk coverage expanded** (TECH-6): business-hours-only to full 24/7/365, a materially larger staffing commitment.
- **Schedule slipped roughly 3 months** across every post-award milestone (TIME-2, TIME-4, TIME-5).
