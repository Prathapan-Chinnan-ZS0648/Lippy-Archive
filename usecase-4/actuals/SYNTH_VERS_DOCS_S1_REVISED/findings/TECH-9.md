> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S1_ORIGINAL.pdf, page 5

## New

## What changed
SSO protocol support widened to include OpenID Connect (OIDC) as an alternative to SAML 2.0.

## Why it matters
Gives the vendor more flexibility in its identity integration approach; not a new mandatory burden.
