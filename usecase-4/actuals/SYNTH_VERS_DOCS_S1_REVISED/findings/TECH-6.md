# TECH-6

**5.4 Hosting, Availability & Security** — CHANGED (material)

## Old
> Deployment model: cloud-hosted or on-premise — vendor may propose either model with technical and cost justification.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S1_ORIGINAL.pdf, page 5

## New
> Deployment model: cloud-hosted only, using AWS or Google Cloud Platform. On-premise deployment will not be considered.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S1_REVISED.pdf, page 5

## What changed
On-premise deployment option removed; cloud-only, restricted to AWS or GCP.

## Why it matters
Vendors who proposed on-premise in the original round are now ineligible on this point and must re-architect around AWS/GCP; also reflected in Appendix C row APPC-6.
