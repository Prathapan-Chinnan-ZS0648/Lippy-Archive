# ELIG-3
**Eligibility Criteria — CHANGED**

## Old
> Reference projects (5 MW+) | 3, completed within 5 years
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S5_ORIGINAL.pptx, Eligibility Criteria, page 7

## New
> Reference projects (5 MW+) | 4, completed within 5 years, ≥1 incl. EV charging
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S5_REVISED.pptx, Eligibility Criteria, page 8

## What changed
The reference-project minimum rises from 3 to 4, and at least one reference must now include an EV charging component.

## Why it matters
A vendor with strong solar references but no EV charging reference, sufficient under the original, would fail this criterion under the revision.
