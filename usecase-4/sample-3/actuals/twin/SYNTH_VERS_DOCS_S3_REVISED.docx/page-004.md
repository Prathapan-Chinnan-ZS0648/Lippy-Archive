## 3. Scope of Work

- Operate and staff 5 dining halls and 12 retail food locations across campus, with a sixth dining hall to be added at Winthrop Commons in fall 2027.
- Manage meal plan billing and integrate with the University's CBORD student ID card system.
- Provide allergen-friendly menu options and clear nutritional labeling at all dining locations, including a dedicated allergen-free preparation station in each dining hall. [MODIFIED]
- Staff dining operations with approximately 205 full-time-equivalent (FTE) employees. [MODIFIED]
- Implement a local and sustainable sourcing program targeting at least 30% of food spend. [MODIFIED]
- Maintain and service kitchen equipment across all dining facilities.
- Provide catering services for University events, conferences, and athletics, including off-campus catering for up to 15 events per year. [NEW]
- Implement a food recovery and donation program, partnering with local food banks to redirect surplus food. [NEW]

## 4. Operational Requirements

- Point-of-sale (POS) integration with the University's existing CBORD student ID card system.
- Online ordering and a mobile app for menu viewing, mobile ordering, and meal plan balance tracking.
- Nutritional and allergen labeling for all menu items, updated each semester.
- Compliance with all applicable state and local health department codes and inspection requirements.
- At least one dedicated vegetarian/vegan station in each dining hall.
- Dedicated catering event-management staff and online catering order portal for University departments. [NEW]
