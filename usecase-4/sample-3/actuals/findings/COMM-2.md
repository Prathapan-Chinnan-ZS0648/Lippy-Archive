> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S3_ORIGINAL.docx, 10. Commercial Terms, page 7

## New
> Contract Term: 7 years, with one (1) optional 3-year renewal. [MODIFIED]
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S3_REVISED.docx, 10. Commercial Terms, page 7

## What changed
The base contract term rises from 5 to 7 years, and the renewal structure changes from two optional 2-year renewals to one optional 3-year renewal.

## Why it matters
Changes the total possible contract length (original: 5+2+2=9 years maximum; revised: 7+3=10 years maximum) and the vendor's renewal leverage points.
