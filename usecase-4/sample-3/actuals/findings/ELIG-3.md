# ELIG-3
**5. Eligibility Criteria — CHANGED**

## Old
> At least 3 reference accounts at universities with 5,000 or more students, currently active or completed within the past 5 years.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S3_ORIGINAL.docx, 5. Eligibility Criteria, page 5

## New
> At least 4 reference accounts at universities with 5,000 or more students, currently active or completed within the past 5 years, of which at least 1 must include a catering services program. [MODIFIED]
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S3_REVISED.docx, 5. Eligibility Criteria, page 5

## What changed
The reference-account minimum rises from 3 to 4, and at least one reference must now include a catering program.

## Why it matters
A vendor with strong dining references but no catering reference, sufficient under the original, would fail this criterion under the revision.
