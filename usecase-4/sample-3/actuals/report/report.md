## Clauses that need attention before a vendor responds

- **Eligibility bar rises across the board** (`ELIG-1` through `ELIG-4`): years of experience 5→7, minimum revenue $20M→$30M, reference accounts 3→4 with a catering reference now required, insurance minimums both raised. A vendor that qualified under the original RFP may no longer qualify.
- **The liquidated-damages clause reverses a stated absence, not a plain addition** (`COMM-6`): the original states outright that no such clause applies; the revision ties a $5,000/day penalty to the Winthrop Commons dining hall opening date (`TIME-10`). A vendor pricing this engagement off the original's risk profile would materially under-price the revised one.
- **Two changes carry no inline tag at all** (`SCOPE-1`, `SUB-1`): the sixth-dining-hall commitment and the submission email/validity-period change are both substantive but unmarked in the source document's own `[NEW]`/`[MODIFIED]` scheme.
- **Evaluation weights are rebalanced, not merely added to** (`EVAL-1`, `EVAL-2`, `EVAL-6`): 10 points move from Culinary Quality and Vendor Experience into a new Catering Program Design criterion, keeping the total at 100.
- **A renumbering control case** (`DEL-7`): the Annual Dining Services Performance Report is unchanged in substance but moves from Deliverables row 5 to row 7 after two new rows are inserted ahead of it — correctly classified UNCHANGED, not CHANGED.
