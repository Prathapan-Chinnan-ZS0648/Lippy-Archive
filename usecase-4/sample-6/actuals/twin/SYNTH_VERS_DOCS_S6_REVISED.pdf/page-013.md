## 14. Terms & Conditions

### 14.1 Governing Law
This RFP and any resulting contract shall be governed by the laws of the State of Cascadia.

### 14.2 Confidentiality
All proposal materials submitted become the property of CDOT and are subject to applicable public records law, subject to any properly marked trade secret exemptions.

### 14.3 Reservation of Rights
CDOT reserves the right to reject any or all proposals, waive minor irregularities, and negotiate final contract terms with the selected vendor.

### 14.4 Intellectual Property
All custom configurations, integrations, and documentation developed specifically for CDOT under this engagement shall become the property of CDOT upon final payment. Vendor retains ownership of pre-existing, generally licensed software.

### 14.5 Indemnification
Vendor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless CDOT and its officers, agents, and employees from any claims, damages, or losses arising from the vendor's negligent acts or omissions in the performance of this contract.

### 14.6 Dispute Resolution
The parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation before pursuing formal legal remedies. Unresolved disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of the State of Cascadia.

### 14.7 Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, acts of government, or other force majeure events, provided the affected party gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate the delay.

### 14.8 Assignment
Vendor may not assign or subcontract a material portion of this contract without CDOT's prior written consent.

### 14.9 Cybersecurity Incident Notification [NEW]
Vendor must notify CDOT's Chief Information Security Officer within 30 minutes of confirming any intrusion or anomalous activity on the ITS communications network, and must deliver a written incident report within 3 business days of resolution.
