## Appendix F: Sample Service Level Metrics

The following illustrative service levels will inform the O&M support contract to be negotiated with the selected vendor following statewide go-live:

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| ATMS platform uptime | 99.5% monthly |
| Critical field device fault response (on-site) | Within 8 business hours |
| Non-critical field device fault response (on-site) | Within 3 business days |
| Software defect (Severity 1) resolution | Within 24 hours |
| Software defect (Severity 2) resolution | Within 5 business days |
