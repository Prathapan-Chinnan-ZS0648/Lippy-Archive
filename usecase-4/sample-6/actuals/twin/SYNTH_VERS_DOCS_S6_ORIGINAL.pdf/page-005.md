## 4. Scope of Work

1. Design, procure, and deploy a replacement Advanced Traffic Management System (ATMS) software platform.
2. Replace 620 end-of-life CCTV cameras with new pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) IP cameras across all corridors listed in Appendix E.
3. Replace or upgrade 340 dynamic message sign (DMS) controllers, retaining existing sign structures where feasible.
4. Modernize 180 ramp metering locations with adaptive, demand-responsive metering algorithms.
5. Upgrade the fiber and wireless communications backbone connecting all field devices to the TMC.
6. Integrate the new ATMS platform with CDOT's statewide 511 traveler information system.
7. Implement AI-based automated incident detection algorithms across all CCTV-covered corridors.
8. Provide TMC operator training for approximately 45 operators across three shifts.
9. Harden ITS network cybersecurity in alignment with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.
10. Provide 5 years of post-deployment operations and maintenance (O&M) support.
