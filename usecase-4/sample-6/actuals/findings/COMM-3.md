# COMM-3
**12. Commercial Terms — CHANGED**

## Old
> 10% upon contract signing / NTP
> 15% upon System Design Document acceptance
> 20% upon Phase 1 Pilot Corridor go-live
> 25% upon Phase 2 Regional Rollout go-live
> 20% upon Phase 3 Statewide Rollout go-live
> 10% upon final acceptance
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_ORIGINAL.pdf, 12. Commercial Terms, page 11

## New
> 10% upon contract signing / NTP
> 10% upon System Design Document acceptance
> 10% upon SOC monitoring go-live [NEW]
> 20% upon Phase 1 Pilot Corridor go-live
> 25% upon Phase 2 Regional Rollout go-live
> 15% upon Phase 3 Statewide Rollout go-live
> 10% upon final acceptance
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_REVISED.pdf, 12. Commercial Terms, page 11

## What changed
The payment schedule is restructured from 6 lines to 7: a new 10% milestone tied to SOC monitoring go-live is inserted, the System Design Document milestone drops from 15% to 10%, and the Phase 3 milestone drops from 20% to 15%, keeping the total at 100%.

## Why it matters
A vendor's cash-flow planning across the contract must be rebuilt around the new milestone structure, not just around the new SOC go-live date at TIME-8.
