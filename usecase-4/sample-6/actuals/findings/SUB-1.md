# SUB-1
**13. Submission Instructions — CHANGED**

## Old
> Proposals must be submitted electronically in PDF format to its-procurement@cdot.state.gov no later than the deadline listed above. Proposals must remain valid for 150 days from the submission deadline. Late submissions will not be considered.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_ORIGINAL.pdf, 13. Submission Instructions, page 12

## New
> Proposals must be submitted electronically in PDF format to its-proposals@cdot.state.gov no later than the deadline listed above. Proposals must remain valid for 180 days from the submission deadline. Late submissions will not be considered.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_REVISED.pdf, 13. Submission Instructions, page 12

## What changed
The submission email address changes from its-procurement@cdot.state.gov to its-proposals@cdot.state.gov, and the proposal-validity period extends from 150 to 180 days. Neither change carries a [MODIFIED] marker.

## Why it matters
A vendor sending its proposal to the original address risks it going to the wrong inbox; the validity-period extension binds a vendor's pricing for a full month longer. Both changes are untagged, so a reader relying on the document's own markers would miss both — the pack's untagged-silent-change case, alongside SCOPE-5's untagged SR-509 addition.
