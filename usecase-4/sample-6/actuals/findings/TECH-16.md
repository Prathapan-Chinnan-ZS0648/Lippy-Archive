# TECH-16
**5.6 Hosting, Availability & Security — CHANGED**

## Old
> Deployment model: CDOT-hosted on-premise at the TMC, or vendor-hosted cloud environment — vendor may propose either model with justification.
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_ORIGINAL.pdf, 5.6 Hosting, Availability & Security, page 6

## New
> Deployment model: vendor-hosted cloud environment only. On-premise TMC hosting will not be considered. [MODIFIED — REMOVES ON-PREM OPTION]
> — SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S6_REVISED.pdf, 5.6 Hosting, Availability & Security, page 6

## What changed
The on-premise hosting option is removed entirely; only a vendor-hosted cloud environment is now permitted.

## Why it matters
Eliminates a deployment path a vendor may have planned to propose; a vendor whose solution depends on on-premise hosting can no longer bid that architecture.
