## Clauses that need attention before a vendor responds

- **A full cybersecurity resilience program is added on top of the original scope** (`OBJ-7`, `SCOPE-10`, `TECH-24`, `TECH-25`, `FR-13`, `FR-14`, `ELIG-5`, `INS-3`, `DEL-10`, `TC-9`, `APPD-5`, `APPA-9`, `APPC-9`, `APPC-10`, `APPF-6`): a 24/7 SOC, two new mandatory functional requirements, a new eligibility gate, a new insurance line, a new deliverable, a new contract clause, and a new technical specification, all tied together. A vendor should read these as one coherent new work stream, not scattered independent additions.
- **The on-premise hosting option is removed entirely** (`TECH-16`, `APPC-10`): a vendor whose solution depends on on-premise TMC hosting can no longer bid that architecture at all.
- **Eligibility and insurance minimums rise across the board** (`ELIG-1` through `ELIG-3`, `ELIG-6`, `INS-1`, `INS-2`): years of experience, revenue, references, and both liability minimums all increase; a vendor that qualified under the original may no longer qualify.
- **The schedule slips roughly four months across every downstream phase** (`TIME-9` through `TIME-12`): Phase 1 go-live alone moves from February 2027 to June 2027.
- **Two changes carry no inline tag at all** (`SCOPE-5`, `SUB-1`): the SR-509 corridor addition to the communications-backbone scope, and the submission email/validity-period change, are both substantive but unmarked in the source document's own `[NEW]`/`[MODIFIED]` scheme.
- **The RFP's own Table of Contents page numbers cannot be trusted for citation** (see `bootstrap.md`, `prompting.md`, and `actuals/twin/section-map.md`): every citation in this pack uses the PDF's physical page instead.
- **FR-07 and FR-08 change priority, not text** (`FR-07`, `FR-08`): both move from Desired to Mandatory with their requirement text untouched — a CHANGED classification, not ADDED or REMOVED.
