## What changed
Rev 4 introduces a drawing-specific legend defining an extended meaning for its revision clouds: a cloud carrying an asterisk "(*)" marks changes already communicated to the fabricator outside this formal reissue. The sheet's own Rev-4 clouds honour this: "(*)" appears at three of the seven "4" balloons — (534,385) on the T171 (-175) member cloud, (1367,393) on the upper dimension-band cloud, (1363,1373) on the lower dimension-band cloud.

## Why it matters
material=no to fabrication (it defines no geometry), but it is a required read: per the skill rule, the drawing's own cloud-marker convention is a NOTE-shape unit and must be reported, not assumed. It changes how a reader triages Rev-4 clouds — asterisked clouds are "old news already conveyed," unasterisked "4" clouds (e.g. at (562,660), (453,822), (561,1129), (714,1322)) are the genuinely new items of this issue. A run that treats every cloud as equally new — or that never reads the legend — mis-serves the fabricator.
