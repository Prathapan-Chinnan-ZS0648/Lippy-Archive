# Price

**What is the final evaluated price as the record states it? Where the record separates a base (pre-tax) figure from a tax-inclusive total under any labels, which figure is the tax-inclusive total, and which is the component base figure?**

STATED

## What the record says

**Final Total Bid Value (Stage 2): ₹32,60,59,217** (Final Total Base Price
₹27,63,21,370 + GST @ 18% ₹4,97,37,847) — no base price revision offered at Stage 2.

The record separately notes a conforming-alternate price addendum (Siemens relay OEM +
40kA-rated breaker) at a 6.2% increase over the quoted base price, submitted by the
bidder "to be considered only if the non-compliant items ... are rejected by the
Evaluation Committee." Per Module 1's rule on a normalized/comparison figure standing
beside the quoted one, this addendum is mentioned here as the committee's working; the
quoted contract price above is the price.

and the separate conditional addendum is correctly identified as working detail rather
than a competing price.
