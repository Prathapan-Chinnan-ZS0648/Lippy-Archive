# Basis

**Why does this determination follow from the record?**

STATED

## What the record says

The Eligibility Compliance Statement (Section 2) records compliance on seven of eight
criteria with one criterion (EC-3) qualified-with-concern rather than failed — per the
skill's rule, this line reports the committee's own per-criterion status, and the
bidder's overall determination ("TECHNICALLY QUALIFIED WITH MAJOR DEVIATIONS," not
disqualified) is what states the outcome, not a count of imperfect criteria. The
Deviations table (Section 24) records two major, potentially-disqualifying technical
deviations and one favorable schedule deviation, each with the committee's own
assessment quoted.

The Evaluation Committee Remarks (Section 32) independently characterize this as
carrying the "lowest quoted Combined Cost among all bidders" — **this directly conflicts
with the priced BOQ's own Combined Evaluated Cost figure verified in the Price field**
(₹1,41,07,52,89,990, roughly 48x S1's and S3's), a discrepancy this finding surfaces
rather than resolves: it is not this skill's place to decide whether the committee's
remark predates the RGF pricing anomaly, refers to a different cost basis, or the priced
BOQ itself is in error — that adjudication belongs to the Evaluation Committee, not to
this reading of the record. The Remarks otherwise note "two major, potentially
disqualifying technical deviations... require Evaluation Committee adjudication," with
O&M experience "just meet[ing] the 3-year minimum with no margin."

## Why this verdict

The record supports the Score field's determination with reasoning drawn from three
sections, though one section's own figure (lowest Combined Cost) conflicts with the
verified Price field — that conflict is itself reported, not resolved by this skill.
