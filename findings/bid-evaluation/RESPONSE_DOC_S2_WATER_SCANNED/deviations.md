# Deviations

**What deviations did the committee flag?**

STATED

## What the record says

Three:

1. Sodium hypochlorite dosing proposed in place of gas chlorination, not clearly
   flagged as a deviation in the Bid Submission Form as required by ITB-10 — assessed
   "Major - technically permissible... but the failure to explicitly declare it is
   itself a procedural non-compliance requiring Evaluation Committee determination."
2. Filter media depth (sand 55cm, anthracite 25cm) both below the specified minimum
   ranges — assessed "Major - directly affects filtered water quality and filter run
   length; not acceptable without redesign or explicit, justified deviation with
   performance guarantee."
3. Compressed execution schedule of 552 days (92% of the 600-day base) — assessed
   "Positive deviation (favorable) - subject to verification of resource loading
   feasibility."

## Why this verdict

The record states three deviations in full, each with the committee's own assessment
quoted.
