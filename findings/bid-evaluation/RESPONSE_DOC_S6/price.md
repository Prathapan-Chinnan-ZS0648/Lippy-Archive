# Price

**What is the final evaluated price the record states for this bidder?**

STATED

## What the record says

₹22,87,38,000 — the Final Total Bid Value: the quoted Contract Price of ₹20,10,00,000
plus GST @ 13.8% of ₹2,77,38,000, consistent with how every other bidder's price is
reported in this tender. The record separately names two base-price figures, distinctly:
the quoted Contract Price (₹20,10,00,000, unchanged) and the committee's Normalized
Evaluated Base Price (₹19,20,00,000, crediting voluntary O&M value-addition). Stage 1:
Total Base Price ₹20,10,00,000 | GST @ 13.8% ₹2,77,38,000 | Total Bid Value
₹22,87,38,000. Stage 2: no price revision; Evaluation Committee separately computed a
Normalized Evaluated Base Price of ₹19,20,00,000 for financial comparison purposes only
(quoted ₹20,10,00,000 less ₹0,90,00,000 value-addition credit); the quoted Contract Price
itself remains unchanged. Final Total Bid Value: ₹22,87,38,000.

## Why this verdict

The record separates a quoted base figure from a tax-inclusive total, and further from a
committee normalization figure — per Module 3, the final tax-inclusive quoted total
(₹22,87,38,000) is the price; the Normalized Evaluated Base Price is explicitly the
committee's own comparison working, never the price itself, and the narrower Contract
Price is a component of the same quoted total, not a separate lower price.
