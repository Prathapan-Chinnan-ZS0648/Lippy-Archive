# Deviations

**What deviations did the committee flag?**

STATED

## What the record says

Not applicable as a separate deviations table in the ordinary sense: the record's
Section 24 restates the four eligibility failures themselves (turnover, O&M experience,
ISO 45001, EMD), each assessed "Major - Disqualifying" (the EMD shortfall assessed
"Major - Non-responsive per ITB-13 unless rectified within cure period"). The bidder's
clarification responses (Section 25) did not cure any of the four grounds: the cited
O&M assignments were "not accepted" as satisfying EC-3's continuity requirement, no
additional EMD was received within the cure period, and no valid ISO 45001 certificate
could be provided before bid evaluation closure.

## Why this verdict

The record states this field's content in full, even though it is structurally embedded
in the eligibility record rather than presented as a separate deviations table — the
content itself is present, so this is STATED, not NOT APPLICABLE (which would require
the deviations question's own precondition never to have arisen at all).
