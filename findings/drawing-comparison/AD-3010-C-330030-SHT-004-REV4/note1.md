## What changed
A new legend defines what an asterisk on a Rev 4 revision cloud means. `MEMB-1` is the only Rev 4 cloud on this sheet marked with `(*)`.

## Why it matters
Not itself a fabrication fact, but it changes how a reader should treat the sheet's own revision clouds: a `(*)`-marked cloud is not "new news" to the fabricator, while an un-marked (or entirely uncloud) change still is.
