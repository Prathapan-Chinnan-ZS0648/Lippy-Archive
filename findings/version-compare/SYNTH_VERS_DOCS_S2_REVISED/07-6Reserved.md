# Findings — 6. [Reserved]

The original's Section 6, "Vendor Onboarding & Staffing Requirements," is replaced in the
revision by a section retitled "[Reserved]" stating the content was removed and folded
into the MSA. The section number and table-of-contents slot persist across both
documents; the content does not — see `actuals/version-compare/SYNTH_VERS_DOCS_S2_REVISED/Detection.md`
for the general handling rule.

| Clause | Verdict | Old | New |
|---|---|---|---|
| Section heading | CHANGED | "6. Vendor Onboarding & Staffing Requirements" | "6. [Reserved]" |
| Background-check requirement | REMOVED | "All vendor personnel with access to AFG systems must pass a background check consistent with FFIEC guidance prior to engagement." | NOT ADDRESSED — searched for and not found anywhere in the revised document |
| Security-training requirement | REMOVED | "Vendor staff must complete AFG's security awareness training within 5 business days of assignment." | NOT ADDRESSED — searched for and not found |
| Staffing-roster notification requirement | REMOVED | "Vendor must maintain a current staffing roster and notify AFG of any personnel changes within 2 business days." | NOT ADDRESSED — searched for and not found |
| Physical access control requirement | REMOVED | "On-site vendor personnel must badge in and out through AFG's physical access control system." | NOT ADDRESSED — searched for and not found |
| Subcontracting approval requirement | REMOVED | "Vendor may not subcontract any portion of the helpdesk service without prior written approval from AFG." | NOT ADDRESSED — searched for and not found |
| Reservation explanatory note | ADDED | NOT ADDRESSED | "This section (previously \"Vendor Onboarding & Staffing Requirements\" in RFP-AFG-2026-0064) has been removed from this revision. Vendor personnel screening, security training, staffing roster notification, physical access control, and subcontracting approval requirements are now governed exclusively by the Master Services Agreement (MSA) executed at contract award. Vendors should not include responses to this section in their proposal." |

Note: the new explanatory note asserts that the removed requirements are "now governed
exclusively by the Master Services Agreement (MSA)." The MSA itself is not one of the two
documents configured for this comparison, so this claim is reported as the revision's own
assertion and cannot itself be verified against MSA text — it is not treated as evidence
that the substance of the requirements survives elsewhere, only that the RFP text does not
state it.
