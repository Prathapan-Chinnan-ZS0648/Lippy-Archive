# Page 5 — Drawing E06, "Overall Pump Station One-Line Diagram"

A second, complementary one-line view of the same system as page 4 (Drawing E05), drawn
from the pump-station perspective, cross-confirming most of page 4's ratings and adding
detail page 4 does not show. Top-right repeats the GSW-2/generator one-line (G3-G5 solid,
**G6 and NGR-G6 dashed/future**, NGR-G3/G4/G5, breakers FG1/FG2/F3, feeding a "**LOAD
BANK 2000KW**" block — confirms the on-site load bank's rating independently of page 9's
elevation view). Top-left repeats TX-M1/TX-M2 (7.5/8.4/9.3/10.5MVA) and MVSWGR-E/MVSWGR-F
("4160V, 3Ø, 4W, 2000A, 50KA SCCR TIN PLATED COPPER BUS" on this sheet), each with 2 spare
1200A breakers.

Below MVSWGR-E/F: four **4.16kV/2000KVA transformers (TX-9, TX-7, TX-8, TX-10)** each with
their own FPR block, feeding a combined **SWB-E and SWB-F** bus arrangement — **480V,
3000A** main buses on each side, tied via **T1/T2 3000A** breakers in the middle, each
main bus also carrying an M1/M2 breaker.

Below the SWB-E/SWB-F bus, four downstream loads: two **VFD(1200HP)** blocks (one solid,
one dashed/grey) feeding "**FUTURE FINISHED WATER PUMP 8**" and "**FUTURE FINISHED WATER
PUMP 6**" respectively (both dashed/grey style, i.e. future — despite the "VFD(1200HP)"
label text itself not saying "future", the box styling and the "FUTURE" prefix on both
pump labels agree); four **VFD(600HP)** blocks feeding, left to right: "**FINISHED WATER
PUMP 6 (RELOCATED)**", "**FINISHED WATER PUMP 7 (RELOCATED)**", **MCC-E**, "**FINISHED
WATER PUMP 8 (RELOCATED)**" — i.e. three existing 600HP-VFD-driven pumps (6, 7, 8) are
being relocated as part of this project, distinct from the future 1200HP-VFD provisions
for (apparently) the same pump numbers 6 and 8 reserved for a later capacity increase.
This pump-number reuse (6 and 8 appearing both as "(RELOCATED)" 600HP loads and as
"FUTURE" 1200HP stubs) is recorded as-is, not reconciled — see `detection.md`.

PRE-PURCHASE PACKAGE."
