# Page 1 — Drawing GE01, "Legend and Symbols - I"

A four-column symbols/description legend sheet (columns 1-4, 5-6, 7-8, 9-10, plus a
General Note column). Defines the drawing set's standard one-line/schematic symbols:
medium-voltage drawout power circuit breakers, low-voltage circuit breakers (LSIG/MCP/
ERMS options), combination motor circuit protector + magnetic motor starter (FVNR/FVR/
RVNR/2S1W/2S2W), non-fusible and fusible disconnect switches, motor isolation switches
(MIS), drawout-type equipment, medium-voltage cable termination/air interrupter switch/
fused air interrupter switch/fused motor controller, dry-type transformer symbol (with a
note: "ALL DRY TYPE TRANSFORMERS SERVICING 480V- ... ADMINISTRATIVE AND LIFE SAFETY
LOADS SHALL HAVE A K-FACTOR OF 13, ALL OTHER DRY TYPE TRANSFORMERS SERVICING ISOLATION
TRANSFORMERS SHALL HAVE A K-20 RATING"), current/potential transformer symbols, generator
symbol, transfer switch symbol (ATS-1), variable speed drive controller (VFD, DC-D.C.
drive controller, SCR silicon controlled rectifier), vacuum fault interrupter, unit
heater symbols, motor symbol, surge protection device.

Right-hand columns: meters (voltmeter, ammeter, wattmeter/watthour meter/watthour demand
meter/power factor meter/running time meter), pilot lights, time delay relay, current/
watt transducers, a long list of numbered protective-relay device functions (25, 27, 38,
40, 42, 46, 47, 49, 50, 50G, 51, 51G, 51N, 51V, 60, 62, 63, 67, 83, 86, 87, with B/G/T/X/
GF/ST suffix meanings), field instrument bubble symbol ("FIELD INSTRUMENT, TAG NO. OR
LOOP # AS INDICATED — INDICATES INSTRUMENT TYPE DEFINED ON LOOP SHEETS; ## INDICATES LOOP
NO."), switch symbols (liquid level, pressure/vacuum, temperature, flow, position/limit,
torque), relay contact symbols, lightning arrester/surge capacitor, ground rod/ground rod
well, fuse, heater, inductor, tachometer generator, terminal/test block, RTD, vibration
detector, damper motor, motor operated valve, junction box variants, pull box, remote
devices, MOV disconnect symbols.

General Note (top-right): "THIS IS A STANDARD LEGEND. SOME SYMBOLS MAY NOT APPEAR ON THE
DRAWINGS." — confirms this legend is a boilerplate sheet shared across GAI/Carollo
drawing sets, not custom-built for this project; not every symbol on it necessarily
appears in this 11-page set.
