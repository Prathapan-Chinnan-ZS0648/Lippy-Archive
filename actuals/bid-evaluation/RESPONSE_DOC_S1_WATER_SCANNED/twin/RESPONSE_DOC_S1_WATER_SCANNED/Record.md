5. Design Basis & Engineering Philosophy

has carried out preliminary process and engineering studies for the proposed 100
MLD Conventional Water Treatment Plant at Kosad, Surat, based on the design basis parameters, raw
water characteristics data (Annexure L-2), and site data (Annexure L-1) provided in the Tender
Document, supplemented by the bidder's own site visit during the pre-bid period. The design basis
adopted is consistent with the CPHEEO Manual on Water Supply and Treatment, relevant Indian
Standards, and GWSSB's Standard Specifications (Parts L-A and L-B of the BASE_DOC).

The raw water quality data reviewed indicates a wide seasonal variation in turbidity, from approximately
10 NTU in the lean season to over 1000 NTU during monsoon peak flows. The treatment train has
accordingly been sized with adequate flexibility in coagulant dosing (jar-test-optimized) and
clariflocculator surface loading to consistently achieve the guaranteed treated water turbidity of < 1
NTU across this full seasonal range.

The overall plant hydraulic profile has been designed to operate substantially by gravity flow from the
clariflocculator outlet through to the Clear Water Reservoir, minimizing intermediate pumping
requirements and associated power consumption, consistent with the specific power consumption
guarantee of < 0.55 kWh per kL set out in Part H of the BASE_DOC.

Seismic considerations for the Surat region (Zone III per IS 1893) have been incorporated into the
structural design philosophy for all major water-retaining structures, with crack-width control per IS
3370 to ensure long-term watertightness and durability over the specified 30-year civil design life.

The plant layout has been developed to provide adequate operational access for maintenance vehicles
and equipment removal at all major process units, with the chlorination and chemical storage areas
segregated per the safety requirements of Part L-A (CS-14) and located with due consideration to
prevailing wind direction relative to the administrative building and site boundary.

16. Project Execution Methodology (D&B Phase)

proposes to execute this project using a parallel-path methodology wherein civil
works, long-lead equipment procurement (pumps, clariflocculator mechanisms, filter systems,
chlorination equipment), rising main procurement, and detailed engineering proceed concurrently from
Day 1 of mobilization, in order to achieve the approximately 20-month Design-Build schedule while
managing interface risk through a dedicated Planning Engineer using a detailed Level-3 project
schedule. Monthly progress review meetings will be held with GWSSB, supported by weekly internal
progress tracking.

Key execution phases comprise: (i) Mobilization & Engineering, (ii) Civil Works, (iii) Equipment
Procurement & Manufacturing, (iv) Rising Main Laying, (v) Erection & Installation, (vi) Testing, Pre-
Commissioning & Commissioning, (vii) 30-Day Performance Guarantee Test Run, and (viii)
Provisional Acceptance & Handover to O&M. Interfaces between civil and mechanical/electrical
erection teams will be managed through a joint interface schedule reviewed weekly.
