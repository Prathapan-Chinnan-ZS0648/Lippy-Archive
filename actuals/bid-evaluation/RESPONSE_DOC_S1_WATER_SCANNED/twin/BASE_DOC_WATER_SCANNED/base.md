The Contractor shall carry out the complete Design-Build of a 100 MLD Conventional Water Treatment
Plant at Kosad, Surat, together with associated raw water pumping station, clear water pumping station,
8.5 km rising main to the city distribution network, SCADA-based automation, and shall subsequently
operate and maintain the completed Works for a period of 10 years, covering the following broad scope,
described in further detail in the subsections below:

D.1 Detailed Engineering

Detailed engineering, hydraulic design, and process design of all treatment units. The Contractor shall
carry out complete detailed engineering including hydraulic profile calculation across the entire
treatment train, process design of each unit operation, structural design of all civil structures, and
preparation of Good-for-Construction (GFC) drawings for the Employer's review and approval prior to
commencement of any construction activity.

D.2 Raw Water Pumping Station

Intake works, pumps, rising main to WTP. This includes tie-in works at the existing GWSSB intake
well, a new raw water pumping station with the pump configuration specified in Part E.2, and a raw
water rising main conveying water to the WTP inlet works, complete with all civil, mechanical,
electrical and instrumentation works.

D.3 Pre-treatment

Screening, aeration (if required based on raw water quality). Coarse and fine screening shall be provided
at the WTP inlet to remove floating debris; aeration shall be provided if deemed necessary based on the
Contractor's own assessment of raw water quality (particularly during low-flow, high-organic-load
periods), subject to Employer's design review.

D.4 Coagulation-Flocculation

Rapid mix + flocculation basins (clariflocculators). Coagulant (alum or poly-aluminium chloride) shall
be dosed at a rapid mix chamber, followed by flocculation in the flocculation zone of the clariflocculator
units, designed per the criteria in Part E.3 and Annexure L-7 (CPHEEO extract).

D.5 Sedimentation / Clarification

Clariflocculator units with tube settlers. Clarification shall be achieved in the same clariflocculator units
(combined flocculation-clarification design), fitted with tube settler modules to enhance settling
efficiency and reduce the footprint required, per the specification in Part E.3.

D.6 Filtration

Rapid Gravity Sand Filters (RGF) with automatic backwashing. Filtration shall be through dual-media
Rapid Gravity Filters as specified in Part E.4, with fully automatic, PLC-controlled sequential
backwashing to minimize manual intervention and ensure consistent filtered water quality.

===== PAGE 22 =====
D.7 Disinfection

Chlorination system (gas chlorination with scrubber, or sodium hypochlorite as alternative). The primary
disinfection system shall be gas chlorination as specified in Part E.5, with all associated safety systems;
bidders proposing sodium hypochlorite dosing as an alternative shall clearly declare this as a deviation
and justify the equivalent disinfection efficacy and cost implications.

D.8 Sludge Handling

Sludge thickening, mechanical dewatering (centrifuge/filter press), sludge drying beds. Sludge generated
from the clariflocculators shall be thickened, mechanically dewatered to a minimum cake dryness of
20%, with sludge drying beds provided as a backup/overflow system during peak monsoon sludge loads,
per Part E.6.

D.9 Clear Water Reservoir (CWR)

Minimum 4-hour storage capacity at design flow. The CWR shall provide a minimum of 4 hours of
balancing storage at the design flow of 100 MLD, constructed as an RCC underground/semi-
underground water-retaining structure per the civil specifications in Part L-A.

D.10 Clear Water Pumping Station

Pumps to convey treated water via the rising main to the city distribution system. As specified in Part
E.7, the clear water pumping station shall be designed with adequate redundancy to ensure uninterrupted
supply to the city distribution network even during single-pump maintenance/failure scenarios.

D.11 Rising Main

Approximately 8.5 km, MS/DI pipeline as per hydraulic design. The Contractor shall carry out detailed
hydraulic transient (surge) analysis and finalize the rising main alignment, pipe material, diameter and
class, subject to the indicative parameters in Part E.7 and the Employer's approval of the final alignment
(which may traverse public roads requiring additional permissions per Part L-A statutory clearances).

D.12 Electrical Works

HT/LT substation, DG backup, all plant electricals. Complete electrical works from the HT incoming
point through to the last LT distribution point shall be provided, per the specifications in Part L-B (M&E
Standard Specifications) and Part E.8.

D.13 SCADA/Instrumentation

Complete process automation and remote monitoring. A fully integrated SCADA system covering all
process areas, with the architecture and instrumentation requirements specified in Part E.8, shall be
provided, commissioned, and demonstrated to meet the specified 99.5% uptime guarantee prior to
Provisional Acceptance.

Seo Test Protocol Step

2 Calibration verification of all critical instruments (flow meters, turbidity/chlorine analyzers, level

3 Confirmation of chemical stock (alum, chlorine, polyelectrolyte) sufficient for the full test duration

4 Confirmation of manpower deployment as per the approved O&M staffing plan (Part H.1)

Annexure L-1 - Site Data Report

The Site for the proposed WTP is located at Village Kosad, on the eastern outskirts of Surat city,
approximately 12 km from the existing GWSSB intake well on the Tapi River. The site comprises
approximately 18 hectares of land, largely flat with a gentle slope towards the south-east, currently
under GWSSB's ownership and free from encroachment as of the date of issue of this Tender Document.

Preliminary geotechnical investigation carried out by the Employer indicates a predominantly alluvial
soil profile, with a safe bearing capacity in the range of 12-18 t/sqm at a founding depth of 1.5-2.5m
below existing ground level, though the Contractor shall be required to carry out its own detailed
geotechnical investigation, including bore holes at all major structure locations, prior to finalizing
foundation design. The groundwater table was recorded at approximately 3.5m below ground level
during the pre-monsoon survey and may rise closer to the surface during the monsoon season, requiring
dewatering provisions during construction of below-ground structures.

The site is accessible via an existing 12m-wide approach road connecting to the Surat-Kosad state
highway, approximately 800m from the site boundary; the Contractor shall be responsible for any
strengthening of this approach road required to support heavy equipment and material transport during
construction, subject to the Employer's approval of the scope and cost of such strengthening as a
reimbursable item.

Grid power is available in the vicinity via an existing 11kV feeder approximately 1.2 km from the site
boundary; the Contractor shall coordinate with the local Discom for the necessary HT connection
augmentation and shall bear the cost of extending the feeder to the site boundary, with connection
charges reimbursed by the Employer as a pass-through cost under the Contract.

No forest land, wildlife sanctuary, or other environmentally sensitive area is located within or adjacent
to the project site; however, the site lies within 500m of the Tapi River floodplain, and the Contractor's
design shall account for the 100-year flood level as provided in the hydrological data available with the
Employer's Irrigation Department, ensuring all critical electrical and control equipment is located above
this flood level.
