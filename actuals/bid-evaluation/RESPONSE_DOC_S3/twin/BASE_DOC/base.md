## 1. Scope of Work

The Contractor shall be responsible for the complete Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) of a 5 MW (AC) Grid-Connected Solar Photovoltaic Power Plant, on a turnkey basis, at the designated site situated at Village Bhachau, Kutch District, Gujraat, including but not limited to:

- Site survey, geotechnical investigation, and detailed engineering design
- Supply of PV modules, inverters, mounting structures, cabling, and Balance of System (BOS) components
- Civil, structural, and electrical works including internal roads, drainage, and fencing
- Construction of 33 kV switchyard and interconnection with the State grid substation
- Installation of SCADA-based remote monitoring and weather station
- Testing, commissioning, and synchronization with the grid
- Comprehensive Operation & Maintenance (O&M) for a period of 5 years post-Commercial Operation Date (COD)
- Training of GSPCL personnel and handover of As-Built documentation
