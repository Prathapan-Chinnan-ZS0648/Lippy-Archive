(Compliant/Non-Compliant), (2) Technical Evaluation out of 100 marks with a qualifying
minimum of 60, and (3) Price Evaluation of technically qualified bidders only, with L1
determined on the Total Evaluated Price after accounting for any normalization for
deviations.
