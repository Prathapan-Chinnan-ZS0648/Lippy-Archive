2. Project Objectives

Provide high-quality, diverse dining options across all campus dining halls and retail locations.

Improve allergen transparency and accommodation for students with dietary restrictions.

Increase the use of local and sustainably sourced ingredients.

Maintain student meal plan satisfaction scores of 80% or higher, as measured by the University's annual dining survey.

Establish a campus-wide food recovery and donation program to reduce food waste. [NEW]
