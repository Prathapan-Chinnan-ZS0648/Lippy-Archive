1. Introduction & Background

Northfield University (“the University”) is a private university with approximately 8,900 undergraduate and graduate students, following stronger-than-expected fall 2026 enrollment and the planned opening of a sixth residence hall, Winthrop Commons, in fall 2027. The University operates 5 dining halls and 12 retail food locations across its main campus, with a sixth dining hall planned to open alongside Winthrop Commons. The University's current dining services contract expires on June 30, 2026, and the University is reissuing this Request for Proposal (RFP) to select a contracted dining services operator for the next contract term, with an expanded scope covering campus catering services.
