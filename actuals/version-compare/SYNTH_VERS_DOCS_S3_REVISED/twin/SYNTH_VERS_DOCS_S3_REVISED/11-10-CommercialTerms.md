10. Commercial Terms

Contract Structure: Revenue-share / commission model, with the University receiving a percentage of gross dining and catering revenue.

Contract Term: 7 years, with one (1) optional 3-year renewal. [MODIFIED]

Minimum guaranteed annual commission to the University: $650,000. [MODIFIED]

Vendor responsible for all kitchen equipment maintenance and replacement during the contract term, including new equipment for the Winthrop Commons dining hall. [MODIFIED]

Payment terms: commission remitted to the University monthly, by the 10th of the following month. [MODIFIED]

Liquidated damages: $5,000 per day for failure to open the Winthrop Commons dining hall by the contractual opening date. [NEW]
