9. Insurance Requirements

Commercial General Liability: $8,000,000 per occurrence. [MODIFIED]

Professional Liability: $3,000,000 per claim. [MODIFIED]

Workers' Compensation: as required by state law.

Liquor Liability: $2,000,000 per occurrence, required for catering events serving alcohol. [NEW]

The University and its trustees, officers, and employees must be named as additional insureds.
