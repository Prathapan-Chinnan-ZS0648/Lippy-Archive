```text
5. Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for consideration, vendors must meet all of the following minimum criteria:

Minimum of 7 years of experience operating contracted dining services at higher education institutions. [MODIFIED]

Minimum annual revenue of $30,000,000 (audited financials for the most recent fiscal year required). [MODIFIED]

At least 4 reference accounts at universities with 5,000 or more students, currently active or completed within the past 5 years, of which at least 1 must include a catering services program. [MODIFIED]

Minimum insurance coverage: $8,000,000 general liability; $3,000,000 professional liability. [MODIFIED]
```
