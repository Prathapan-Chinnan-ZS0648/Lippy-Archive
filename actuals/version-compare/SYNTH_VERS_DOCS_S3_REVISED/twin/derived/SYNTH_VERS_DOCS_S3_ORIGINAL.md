NORTHFIELD UNIVERSITY

Office of Auxiliary Services & Procurement

Campus Dining Services & Meal Plan Management Program

A multi-year auxiliary services contract for dining hall and retail food operations

## 02-1-IntroductionBackground

1. Introduction & Background

Northfield University (“the University”) is a private university with approximately 8,200 undergraduate and graduate students. The University operates 5 dining halls and 12 retail food locations across its main campus. The University's current dining services contract expires on June 30, 2026, and the University is issuing this Request for Proposal (RFP) to select a contracted dining services operator for the next contract term.

## 03-2-ProjectObjectives

2. Project Objectives

Provide high-quality, diverse dining options across all campus dining halls and retail locations.

Improve allergen transparency and accommodation for students with dietary restrictions.

Increase the use of local and sustainably sourced ingredients.

Maintain student meal plan satisfaction scores of 80% or higher, as measured by the University's annual dining survey.

## 04-3-ScopeOfWork

3. Scope of Work

Operate and staff 5 dining halls and 12 retail food locations across campus.

Manage meal plan billing and integrate with the University's CBORD student ID card system.

Provide allergen-friendly menu options and clear nutritional labeling at all dining locations.

Staff dining operations with approximately 180 full-time-equivalent (FTE) employees.

Implement a local and sustainable sourcing program targeting at least 20% of food spend.

Maintain and service kitchen equipment across all dining facilities.

## 05-4-OperationalRequirements

4. Operational Requirements

Point-of-sale (POS) integration with the University's existing CBORD student ID card system.

Online ordering and a mobile app for menu viewing, mobile ordering, and meal plan balance tracking.

Nutritional and allergen labeling for all menu items, updated each semester.

Compliance with all applicable state and local health department codes and inspection requirements.

At least one dedicated vegetarian/vegan station in each dining hall.

## 06-5-EligibilityCriteria

5. Eligibility Criteria


Minimum of 5 years of experience operating contracted dining services at higher education institutions.

Minimum annual revenue of $20,000,000 (audited financials for the most recent fiscal year required).

At least 3 reference accounts at universities with 5,000 or more students, currently active or completed within the past 5 years.

Minimum insurance coverage: $5,000,000 general liability; $2,000,000 professional liability.

## 11-10-CommercialTerms

10. Commercial Terms

Contract Structure: Revenue-share / commission model, with the University receiving a percentage of gross dining revenue.

Contract Term: 5 years, with two (2) optional 2-year renewals.

Minimum guaranteed annual commission to the University: $450,000.

Vendor responsible for all kitchen equipment maintenance and replacement during the contract term.

Payment terms: commission remitted to the University monthly, by the 15th of the following month.

No liquidated damages clause applies to this engagement.
