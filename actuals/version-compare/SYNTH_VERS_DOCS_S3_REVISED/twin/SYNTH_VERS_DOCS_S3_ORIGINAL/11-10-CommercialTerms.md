10. Commercial Terms

Contract Structure: Revenue-share / commission model, with the University receiving a percentage of gross dining revenue.

Contract Term: 5 years, with two (2) optional 2-year renewals.

Minimum guaranteed annual commission to the University: $450,000.

Vendor responsible for all kitchen equipment maintenance and replacement during the contract term.

Payment terms: commission remitted to the University monthly, by the 15th of the following month.

No liquidated damages clause applies to this engagement.
