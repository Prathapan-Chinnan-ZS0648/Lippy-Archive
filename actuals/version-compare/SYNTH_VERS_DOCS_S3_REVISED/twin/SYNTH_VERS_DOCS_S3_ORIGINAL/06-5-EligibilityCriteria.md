```text
5. Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for consideration, vendors must meet all of the following minimum criteria:

Minimum of 5 years of experience operating contracted dining services at higher education institutions.

Minimum annual revenue of $20,000,000 (audited financials for the most recent fiscal year required).

At least 3 reference accounts at universities with 5,000 or more students, currently active or completed within the past 5 years.

Minimum insurance coverage: $5,000,000 general liability; $2,000,000 professional liability.
```
