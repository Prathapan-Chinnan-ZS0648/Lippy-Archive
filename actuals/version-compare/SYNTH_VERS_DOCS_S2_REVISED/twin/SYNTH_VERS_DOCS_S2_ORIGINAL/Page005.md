5. Technical Requirements

5.1 ITSM Platform
   • Alignment with ITIL v4 service management practices.
   • Built-in configuration management database (CMDB) with automated asset discovery.
   • Self-service portal accessible via web browser and a companion mobile app.
   • Configurable workflow automation for common request types (password resets, access requests, hardware
      requests).

5.2 Helpdesk Operations
   • Tier 1 and Tier 2 helpdesk coverage during AFG business hours, 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM Eastern Time, Monday
      through Friday.
   • Multichannel intake: phone, email, and self-service portal.
   • First-contact resolution target of 70% or higher for Tier 1 tickets.

5.3 Hosting, Availability & Security
   • Deployment model: cloud-hosted or on-premise — vendor may propose either model with technical and cost
      justification.
   • System uptime service level agreement (SLA) of 99.5% or higher, measured monthly.
   • Single sign-on (SSO) support via SAML 2.0, integrated with AFG's existing Azure Active Directory.
   • Encryption at rest (AES-256 or equivalent) and in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher).
   • Ticket and activity data retention of a minimum of 3 years.

5.4 System Integrations
   • SSO integration with AFG's existing Azure Active Directory environment.
   • Read-only data export capability to AFG's core banking platform (FIS) for asset-to-account correlation reporting.

5.5 Vendor Support Responsibilities
The selected vendor's ongoing support obligations shall include the following:
   • Maintain a dedicated Tier 1 helpdesk available during AFG business hours for password resets and account
      lockouts.
   • Provide a named Technical Account Manager (TAM) as a single point of contact for service escalations.
   • Deliver monthly service performance reports covering ticket volume, resolution time, and SLA compliance.
   • Maintain a knowledge base of at least 200 self-service articles, updated quarterly.
   • Provide on-site support coverage at AFG's headquarters campus during business hours.
