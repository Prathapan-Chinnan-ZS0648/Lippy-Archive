6. Vendor Onboarding & Staffing Requirements

All vendor personnel assigned to this engagement are subject to the following onboarding and staffing requirements:
   • All vendor personnel with access to AFG systems must pass a background check consistent with FFIEC guidance
      prior to engagement.
   • Vendor staff must complete AFG's security awareness training within 5 business days of assignment.
   • Vendor must maintain a current staffing roster and notify AFG of any personnel changes within 2 business days.
   • On-site vendor personnel must badge in and out through AFG's physical access control system.
   • Vendor may not subcontract any portion of the helpdesk service without prior written approval from AFG.
