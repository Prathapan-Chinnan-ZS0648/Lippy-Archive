1. Introduction & Background

Ashford Financial Group (“AFG” or “the Company”) is a regional bank holding company operating 46 branch locations
across five states (Pennsylvania, Ohio, New York, New Jersey, and Delaware), with approximately 2,300 employees.
AFG's current IT ticketing platform, TrackIT Legacy, was deployed in 2014 and no longer meets the Company's needs
for workflow automation, self-service, and reporting.
AFG is issuing this Request for Proposal (RFP) to solicit qualified vendors to replace its legacy ticketing platform with a
modern IT Service Management (ITSM) solution and to provide outsourced Tier 1 and Tier 2 helpdesk support
services.


2. Definitions

 Term                         Definition

                              requests, incidents, and changes.

 Helpdesk                     The vendor-operated support function that receives, triages, and resolves

3. Project Objectives

   • Replace the legacy TrackIT ticketing platform with a modern, ITIL v4-aligned ITSM solution.
   • Establish outsourced Tier 1 and Tier 2 helpdesk support to improve first-contact resolution rates.
   • Reduce average ticket resolution time by at least 25% within 12 months of go-live.
   • Provide employees with a modern self-service portal and knowledge base.
   • Maintain compliance with applicable FFIEC guidance on third-party technology service providers throughout
        the engagement.


4. Scope of Work

   1. Design, configure, and deploy a replacement ITSM platform covering incident, request, problem, and change
        management.
   2. Provide outsourced Tier 1 and Tier 2 helpdesk support for approximately 2,300 AFG employees across 46
        branch and corporate locations.
   3. Migrate historical ticket data and the configuration management database (CMDB) from the legacy TrackIT
        platform.
   4. Deploy a self-service employee portal with a searchable knowledge base.

5. Technical Requirements

5.1 ITSM Platform
   • Alignment with ITIL v4 service management practices.
   • Built-in configuration management database (CMDB) with automated asset discovery.
   • Self-service portal accessible via web browser and a companion mobile app.
   • Configurable workflow automation for common request types (password resets, access requests, hardware
      requests).

5.2 Helpdesk Operations
   • Tier 1 and Tier 2 helpdesk coverage during AFG business hours, 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM Eastern Time, Monday
      through Friday.
   • Multichannel intake: phone, email, and self-service portal.
   • First-contact resolution target of 70% or higher for Tier 1 tickets.

      justification.
   • System uptime service level agreement (SLA) of 99.5% or higher, measured monthly.
   • Single sign-on (SSO) support via SAML 2.0, integrated with AFG's existing Azure Active Directory.
   • Ticket and activity data retention of a minimum of 3 years.

   • SSO integration with AFG's existing Azure Active Directory environment.
   • Read-only data export capability to AFG's core banking platform (FIS) for asset-to-account correlation reporting.

5.5 Vendor Support Responsibilities
The selected vendor's ongoing support obligations shall include the following:
   • Maintain a dedicated Tier 1 helpdesk available during AFG business hours for password resets and account
      lockouts.
   • Provide a named Technical Account Manager (TAM) as a single point of contact for service escalations.
   • Deliver monthly service performance reports covering ticket volume, resolution time, and SLA compliance.
   • Maintain a knowledge base of at least 200 self-service articles, updated quarterly.
   • Provide on-site support coverage at AFG's headquarters campus during business hours.

6. Vendor Onboarding & Staffing Requirements

All vendor personnel assigned to this engagement are subject to the following onboarding and staffing requirements:
   • All vendor personnel with access to AFG systems must pass a background check consistent with FFIEC guidance
      prior to engagement.
   • Vendor staff must complete AFG's security awareness training within 5 business days of assignment.
   • Vendor must maintain a current staffing roster and notify AFG of any personnel changes within 2 business days.
   • On-site vendor personnel must badge in and out through AFG's physical access control system.
   • Vendor may not subcontract any portion of the helpdesk service without prior written approval from AFG.
