1. Introduction & Background

Ashford Financial Group (“AFG” or “the Company”) is a regional bank holding company operating 55 branch locations
across six states (Pennsylvania, Ohio, New York, New Jersey, Delaware, and Maryland), with approximately 2,650
employees, following the February 2026 acquisition of Colonial Trust Bank, which added 9 branch locations in
Maryland. AFG's current IT ticketing platform, TrackIT Legacy, was deployed in 2014 and no longer meets the
Company's needs for workflow automation, self-service, and reporting.
AFG is reissuing this Request for Proposal (RFP) to solicit qualified vendors to replace its legacy ticketing platform with
a modern IT Service Management (ITSM) solution, provide outsourced Tier 1 and Tier 2 helpdesk support services,
and establish a formal Major Incident Response capability, following a rise in Severity 1 outages observed across the
combined AFG and Colonial Trust environment in the first quarter of 2026.
