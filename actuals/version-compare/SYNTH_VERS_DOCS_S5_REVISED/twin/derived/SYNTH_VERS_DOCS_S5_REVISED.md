## Slide 3: About Solstice Energy Cooperative

Member-owned electric cooperative serving approximately 41,000 members across an expanded 6-county service territory, following the March 2026 addition of the Hartwell service area.
Board of Directors approved a Community Solar + Battery Storage initiative in December 2025, and expanded the program scope in April 2026 to include EV fast-charging infrastructure.
Program goal: add 14 MW of community solar generation, 10 MWh of battery storage, and a public EV fast-charging hub.
This RFP solicits a qualified EPC (Engineer-Procure-Construct) contractor for the expanded program.

## Slide 4: Project Objectives

Project Objectives
Add 14 MW of community solar generation capacity at the Co-op's Meadowbrook site.
Add 10 MWh of battery energy storage for peak shaving and grid support.
Deploy a public DC fast-charging hub at the Meadowbrook site to serve members and the traveling public.
Achieve commercial operation date (COD) by Q2 2028.

## Slide 5: Scope of Work

Scope of Work
Design, procure, and construct a 14 MW solar array at the Meadowbrook site.
Design, procure, and install a 10 MWh battery energy storage system (BESS).
Design, procure, and install a 6-stall DC fast-charging hub at the Meadowbrook site.
Interconnect the solar, BESS, and EV charging systems to the Co-op's existing 34.5kV distribution system.

## Slide 6: Technical Requirements Overview

Battery energy storage: UL 9540 certified, minimum 6-hour discharge duration.
Cybersecurity practices for all networked control systems aligned with NERC CIP guidance.

## Slide 7: EV Charging & Interconnection Requirements

Six DC fast-charging stalls, each rated at a minimum of 150 kW.
OCPP 2.0.1 protocol compliance for charge point management and payment integration.
Charging hub must support future expansion to 12 stalls without additional transformer upgrades.
Dedicated metering for the EV charging hub, separate from the solar and BESS metering points.
