## Slide 3: About Solstice Energy Cooperative

Member-owned electric cooperative serving approximately 38,000 members across a 5-county service territory.
Board of Directors approved a Community Solar + Battery Storage initiative in December 2025.
Program goal: add 12 MW of community solar generation and 8 MWh of battery storage capacity.
This RFP solicits a qualified EPC (Engineer-Procure-Construct) contractor for the program.

## Slide 4: Project Objectives

Project Objectives
Add 12 MW of community solar generation capacity at the Co-op's Meadowbrook site.
Add 8 MWh of battery energy storage for peak shaving and grid support.
Launch a member subscription program allowing members to subscribe to community solar output.
Achieve commercial operation date (COD) by Q4 2027.

## Slide 5: Scope of Work

Scope of Work
Design, procure, and construct a 12 MW solar array at the Meadowbrook site.
Design, procure, and install an 8 MWh battery energy storage system (BESS).
Interconnect the solar and BESS systems to the Co-op's existing 34.5kV distribution system.
Implement a member subscription billing platform integrated with the Co-op's billing system.
Provide 5 years of operations and maintenance (O&M) services following commercial operation.

## Slide 6: Technical Requirements Overview

Solar modules: Tier 1 manufacturer, minimum 21% module efficiency.
Inverters: UL 1741-SA certified with grid-support functions (volt-VAR, frequency-watt).
Battery energy storage: UL 9540 certified, minimum 4-hour discharge duration.
SCADA integration with the Co-op's existing Advanced Distribution Management System (ADMS).
